FAQ

Let's clear up your questions

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope: updating a catalog is not the same as building a store from scratch. After a short conversation I'll send you a proposal with the price and what's included, with no surprises.

How long does it take?

It depends on the project. The proposal includes a specific delivery date, and I share progress along the way.

What do I need to get started?

Tell me what you sell, which channels you use and what you want to improve. If you already have a logo, photos or copy, great; if not, I'll help you organize them.

Can I update my website myself?

Yes. If you need it, I can build a manageable website so you can change text and images without depending on anyone.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm based in Cali, Colombia, and we can talk over WhatsApp, video call or email wherever you are.

What happens after delivery?

I'll walk you through how everything works and stay available for adjustments. I can also take care of ongoing catalog management if you prefer.

Ready for your store to sell more?

Tell me what you need and I'll get back to you with a clear proposal.
